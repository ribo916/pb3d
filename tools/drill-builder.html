How this works

Roster

2, 3, or 4 players. P1 (near) and P3 (far) are always present — the anchors; check or uncheck "include" under P2/P4 to add or remove them, as long as at least one player ends up on each side. Click a player's icon to select them for placement on the court. P1 & P2 are partners on the near side of the net (Team A); P3 & P4 are partners on the far side (Team B) — fixed by every game mode, not a per-drill choice. A shot in the Script panel always goes from a player to one of the opposing team's active players, the way a real rally shot crosses the net — never sideways to your own partner, so the target dropdown only offers opponents.

Positioning

Click anywhere on or around the court to place the selected player — not limited to a fixed grid, so serve positions and ATP/around-the-post spots well outside the sideline work too. The UI only lets you click on that player's own side of the net (near for P1/P2, far for P3/P4) — a click on the wrong side snaps to the nearest legal spot instead of being ignored. Wherever you place a script target within their own side, the engine guarantees they're the one who receives that shot. This positioning is Setup-only, before the rep begins.

Movement cues

Each shot in the script can also carry "moves" — telling any player (the hitter recovering, or a partner poaching/shadowing) to head toward a spot the instant that shot fires. Expand "moves" under a shot row, add a cue, pick who it's for, then click "place on court" and click the target spot. Cues are directions, not guarantees — real AI movement (the same physics-driven steering used everywhere else) carries the player there, and ball responsibility always takes priority if that player needs to actually play the next shot.
